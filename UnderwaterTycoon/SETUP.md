## Step 3 — Configure the Workspace

1. Delete the default **Baseplate** (terrain will be generated by `TerrainSetup`)
2. Set **Workspace → Gravity** to `100` (slightly reduced for underwater feel)
3. Set **Workspace → WaterWaveSpeed** to `0.5`
4. Add a **Lighting** object:
   - `Ambient`: RGB(50, 80, 120)
   - `OutdoorAmbient`: RGB(100, 150, 200)
   - `FogEnd`: 500
   - `FogColor`: RGB(0, 80, 160)

---

## Step 4 — Enable DataStore

1. Go to **Game Settings → Security**
2. Enable **Allow HTTP Requests** ✓
3. Enable **Studio Access to API Services** ✓ (for testing saves in Studio)

---

## Step 5 — Test

1. Press **Play** (F5)
2. The loading screen will appear
3. Select Solo or Multiplayer
4. You'll spawn on the surface boat
5. Walk off the edge of the boat to dive
6. Press **Space** to swim up, **Left Shift** to swim down
7. Click the **🔧 Build** button to open the build menu
8. Click a module to place it (grid snaps automatically)
9. Click a placed module to upgrade/remove it
10. Mineral nodes glow on the ocean floor — swim close and click ⛏ to mine

## Game Systems Overview

### Depth Zones
| Zone | Depth | Unlock Cost | Special |
|------|-------|-------------|---------|
| Sunlight | 0–200m | Free | Coral, crabs, jellyfish |
| Twilight | 200–1000m | 500 Coins | Sharks, eels, needs Pressure Shield |
| Midnight | 1000–4000m | 5,000 Coins | Anglerfish, squid, Kraken boss |
| Abyss | 4000m+ | 50,000 Coins | Megalodon, Sea Titan boss |

### Resource Loop
```
Mine nodes / AFK drills → Minerals
Fish traps → Food → auto-sells for Coins
Air pumps → Oxygen (stops you drowning)
Solar/Thermal → Power (needed for turrets)
Research Lab → Research Points → unlock upgrades
```

### Prestige
Unlock all 4 zones + reach Coin thresholds → Prestige → reset base + get permanent production multiplier

## Notes for Development
- All creatures and bosses have weighted random spawning
- Turrets auto-target within range, no setup needed
- Pressure damage only applies in Zones 2+ without a Pressure Shield nearby
- Boss Kraken spawns every 10min for Zone 3 players; Sea Titan every 30min for Zone 4
- Players can manually summon bosses early by spending Coins (10% of boss reward)
- Research tree has 14 unlockable upgrades across 5 branches
- 5 prestige levels with production multipliers up to 5x
