## Week 1 Survival Guide -- What to Do When You Get 0 Views

This is normal. Every account starts here. Do not panic and do not delete videos.

**If a video gets under 200 views after 24 hours:**
1. Do NOT delete it. TikTok sometimes pushes videos days or weeks later.
2. Check if you used a banned or shadow-limited hashtag. Stick to mid-size hashtags (100K--5M views).
3. Repost the same concept with a different hook in 2--3 days.
4. Your first 1--2 seconds are everything. If retention is bad, film a stronger hook.

**Daily actions (non-negotiable, 30 min/day):**
- Comment genuinely on 20--30 videos in your niche (skincare, wellness, biohacking). Not "nice video!" but real 2--sentence comments that add value or ask a question.
- Reply to every single comment on your own videos within the first hour.
- Watch and engage with content on your For You Page for 10+ minutes so TikTok learns your niche.

**Hashtag strategy:**
- Use 3--5 hashtags per video, not 15
- Mix: 1 broad (#skincare, #wellness), 1 mid (#redlighttherapy, #glowup), 1 specific (#ledmask, #lighttherapy), 1 trending if relevant
- Do NOT use #fyp or #viral. They are useless.

**What "success" looks like in Week 1:**
- 50--500 views per video is perfectly fine
- 1 video breaking 1,000 views is a win
- Gaining 50--200 followers is on track
- If you hit 1 video over 5K views, you are ahead of schedule

**Sound strategy:**
- Use trending sounds when they fit (check the Discover page daily)
- Original audio for talking-head content is fine and can go viral on its own
- Save 5--10 trending sounds each morning to use later

## When to Go Live for the First Time

**Go live once you hit 200+ followers** (this is when you unlock the feature on most accounts).

**Best first live format:** "Ask me anything about red light therapy" or "Watch me do my red light routine."

**Live tips:**
- Go for 20--30 minutes minimum. TikTok pushes longer lives.
- Best times: 7--9 PM your target timezone, or Sunday afternoon.
- Pin a comment that says "Drop a question about skin/red light therapy!"
- Have 5 talking points pre-written so you never run out of things to say.
- Mention "follow for more" naturally every 5 minutes.

## Comment Response Strategy (Algorithm Boost)

Replying to comments signals engagement to TikTok. This is free reach.

**Reply within the first hour of posting.** This is the critical window.

**Types of replies that boost reach:**
1. **Reply with a video.** If someone asks a question, film a reply video. TikTok pushes these hard. Aim for 2--3 reply videos per week.
2. **Ask a follow-up question** in your reply. "Great point -- have you tried red light for that?" This creates a thread.
3. **Pin the most controversial or interesting comment** to the top. This baits more replies.
4. **Like every comment.** Even if you do not reply, like it. Signals engagement.
5. **Never argue.** If someone is negative, reply with "interesting take -- here is what the research says" and stay calm. Controversy in comments actually helps reach.

**Planted engagement:** In early days, ask 2--3 friends to leave genuine comments and questions on each video within the first 15 minutes.

## Batch Filming Days

Four batch filming days are scheduled across the 30 days. On each day, film 10--15 short videos in one session to build a content bank.

| Batch Day | Calendar Day | What to Film |

| Batch 2 | Day 8 (Tuesday) | Week 2 content: comparisons, deeper myth busts, first subtle product appearances |
| Batch 3 | Day 15 (Tuesday) | Week 3 content: product demos, ASMR unboxings, GRWM routines, tutorials |
| Batch 4 | Day 22 (Tuesday) | Week 4 content: urgency posts, testimonial-style, final product showcases |

**Batch filming tips:**

- Film in 2--3 different spots (bathroom mirror, desk, natural light window)
- Batch all your b-roll (product shots, close-ups) at the end
- Edit throughout the week, not all in one day

## Posting Time Strategy

| Time Slot | Best For | Why |
|-----------|---------|-----|
| 7:00--8:00 AM | Educational, quick tips | People scroll before work/school |
| 12:00--1:00 PM | Engaging, shareable | Lunch break scrolling |
| 5:00--6:30 PM | Entertaining, trending | Post-work wind-down |
| 7:00--9:00 PM | Longer content, GRWM, storytime | Peak TikTok hours, highest volume |

Adjust based on YOUR analytics after Week 1. TikTok tells you when your audience is online.

## Key Metrics to Track Weekly

- **Follower growth rate** (aim for 20--30% week-over-week in Month 1)
- **Average view count per video** (track the trend, not individual videos)
- **Save rate** (your best indicator of valuable content -- aim for 2%+)
- **Comment rate** (engagement bait posts should hit 3%+ comment rate)
- **Profile visits from videos** (this shows purchase intent increasing)
- **Link clicks** (track from Week 3 onward)

## Content Pillars for Ongoing Reference

1. **Education** -- Science, how-tos, myth busts, wavelength explanations
2. **Entertainment** -- POV, humor, trending sounds, relatable content
3. **Social Proof** -- Your results, customer results (later), founder journey
4. **Product** -- Demos, comparisons, unboxings, setup guides
5. **Community** -- Q&A, polls, reply videos, engagement posts

Never let any single pillar dominate for more than 2 posts in a row. Rotate constantly.
