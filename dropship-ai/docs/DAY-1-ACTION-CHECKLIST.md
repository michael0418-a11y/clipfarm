## HOUR 1: Accounts & Store

- [ ] Create TikTok account (or switch existing to Business Account)
      Settings > Account > Switch to Business Account > Category: Retail
- [ ] Register as TikTok Shop seller at seller-us.tiktok.com
      You need: SSN or EIN + bank account + phone number
- [ ] Create CJDropshipping account at cjdropshipping.com (free, no card needed)
- [ ] Create free Mailchimp account at mailchimp.com (free up to 500 contacts)
- [ ] Create free Canva account at canva.com
- [ ] Download CapCut app (free video editor by TikTok)

## HOUR 2: Products & Brand

- [ ] On CJDropshipping, search for these products:
      - "LED face mask red light therapy" → pick one similar to our $199 listing
      - "Red light therapy panel" → pick one similar to our $449 listing
      - "Red light therapy wand portable" → pick one similar to our $129 listing
      - "LED eye mask treatment" → pick one for $89
- [ ] DOWNLOAD all supplier product photos AND videos from each CJ listing
      (right-click save — you'll use these for TikTok content)
- [ ] Set up TikTok Shop listings using copy from:
      docs/novara-tiktok-shop-listings.md
- [ ] Logo is already made — use the SVG files in:
      docs/assets/novara-logo.svg (light background)

## HOUR 3: Content (No Filming — Use Supplier Videos)

- [ ] Optimize your TikTok bio:
      "Clinical-grade light therapy. Curated for people who read the research."
      Link: your TikTok Shop URL

- [ ] Create your first 5 videos using supplier footage:

      VIDEO 1 — Text-Over-Video (easiest)
      Take supplier video of LED mask glowing → add text overlays:
      "Red light therapy is a SCAM... or is it?"
      Add trending sound → post

      VIDEO 2 — Photo Carousel
      Download 5 product photos from CJ → TikTok photo mode
      Slide 1: "5 things about red light therapy that blew my mind"
      Slide 2-5: One fact per slide with product photo
      Add trending carousel audio → post

      VIDEO 3 — Green Screen (if comfortable showing face)
      Use TikTok green screen effect → put supplier video behind you
      Talk over it: "I spent $14,000 on facials before finding this"
      OR if no face: just use text-over-video format instead

      VIDEO 4 — Side-by-Side Comparison
      Screenshot a cheap $20 Amazon LED mask AND your product
      Use CapCut split screen → add text:
      "$20 Amazon mask vs clinical-grade — here's the difference"

      VIDEO 5 — Supplier ASMR Repost
      Take the best supplier video showing mask glowing in dark
      Add soft trending audio
      Text overlay: "the most satisfying skincare step"
      No voice, no face, no editing needed

- [ ] Post your first video TODAY
