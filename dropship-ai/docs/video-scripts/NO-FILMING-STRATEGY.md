# No-Filming Content Strategy
## Use existing videos, supplier footage, and stock content

You don't need to film anything yourself. Here's where to get content:

---

## FREE VIDEO SOURCES

### 1. CJDropshipping Product Videos
- Every product listing on CJ has supplier photos AND videos
- Download them directly from the product page
- These show the product in use, unboxing, features, LED glow shots
- **How:** Go to any CJ product page → scroll to images/videos → right-click save

### 2. AliExpress Supplier Videos
- Search "LED face mask red light therapy" on AliExpress
- Suppliers upload high-quality demo videos
- Many include models wearing the mask, showing the glow, etc.
- **How:** Right-click save video from the product listing

### 3. TikTok "Green Screen" Over Images
- Screenshot product photos from CJ/suppliers
- Use TikTok's green screen effect to put yourself in front of them
- Talk OVER the images — you become the narrator/expert
- **This is the #1 strategy for no-filming dropshippers**

### 4. Canva Free Stock Video
- Canva has free stock footage of:
  - Skincare routines
  - Women in spa settings
  - Close-ups of glowing skin
  - Relaxation/self-care scenes
- Layer your voiceover or text overlays on top

### 5. Pexels.com / Pixabay.com (100% Free)
- Search: "skincare routine", "face mask", "spa", "glowing skin"
- Free for commercial use, no attribution needed
- Download and edit in TikTok or CapCut (free app)

### 6. Repost UGC (With Permission)
- Search TikTok for people already reviewing LED masks
- DM them: "Love your video! Can I repost with credit? We'll send you a free [product]"
- Many small creators say yes — they want the exposure

## CONTENT FORMATS THAT DON'T NEED YOUR FACE

### Format A: Green Screen Narrator
**[Your face in corner + product images/videos behind you]**
- Use TikTok green screen effect
- Talk about the product while supplier video plays
- This is how 90% of successful dropshippers post

### Format B: Text-Over-Video
**[Supplier video playing + your text overlays]**
- Take a supplier product video
- Add text overlays with hooks, facts, benefits
- Add trending audio
- No face, no voice needed

### Format C: Photo Carousel with Text
**[Slideshow of product photos + text on each slide]**
- Download 5-8 product photos from CJ
- Make a TikTok carousel (photo mode)
- Add compelling text to each slide
- Add trending audio

### Format D: Screen Recording + Voiceover
**[Screen record yourself browsing the product/reviews]**
- Screen record browsing the CJ product page or your store
- Add voiceover: "I just found this and I need everyone to know..."
- Feels authentic and native to TikTok

### Format E: Comparison Layout
**[Side by side: cheap vs premium product photos]**
- Screenshot cheap Amazon LED masks AND your product
- Use a free collage app or CapCut split screen
- Add text: "$30 mask vs $199 clinical-grade mask — here's the difference"
