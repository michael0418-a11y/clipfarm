# Video Script #2: POV — Your Serums Aren't Working
**Format:** POV / product reveal | **Length:** 20-30 sec | **Difficulty:** Easy

---

## HOOK (0-3 seconds)
**[Text overlay]:** "POV: you finally understand why your $300 serums aren't working"
**[Camera on your bathroom counter showing skincare bottles]**

---

## BODY (3-20 seconds)
**[Pick up first product, look at it, set it down with a "meh" face]**

**Say (casual, like you're talking to a friend):**
"Okay so I had like... twelve serums. Vitamin C, retinol, hyaluronic acid, the whole shelf."

**[Gesture at all the bottles]**

"And my skin was like... fine? But not GOOD. Just... fine."

**[Pause, lean in]**

"Then I learned something. Topical products can only penetrate the top layers of your skin. They literally cannot reach the cells that actually produce collagen."

---

## REVEAL (20-30 seconds)
**[Reach below the counter or behind the products, pull out the LED mask]**

"You know what CAN reach those cells?"

**[Hold up the mask]**

"Light. Specifically, 630 to 850 nanometer light. It goes 2-3mm deep. That's where the magic actually happens."

**[Text overlay]:** "Serums = surface. Light = cellular."

**[Shrug, smile]**

"I still use my serums. But now they actually have something to work WITH."

---

## FILMING NOTES
- Bathroom with decent lighting
- Lay out whatever skincare products you actually own (the messier/more real, the better)
- Film in one continuous take, prop phone against the mirror
- The "reaching for the mask" moment should feel like a natural reveal
- Keep it conversational — imagine you're telling your best friend

## AUDIO
Trending "oh no" or realization sound, OR original audio (your voice)
