# Video Script #3: Storytime — $14,000 on Facials
**Format:** Storytime / talking head | **Length:** 45-60 sec | **Difficulty:** Easy

---

## HOOK (0-3 seconds)
**Say (looking directly at camera, slightly wide-eyed):**
"I spent fourteen thousand dollars on facials before I found out about this."

**[Text overlay]:** "$14,000 on facials"

---

## BODY (3-40 seconds)
**Say:**
"Okay let me break this down."

**[Text overlay as you say each number]:**

"I started getting monthly facials when I was 27. A good facial in my city is like $180. Plus tip, call it $200."

**[Text overlay]:** "$200/month"

"That's $2,400 a year. I did that for almost six years."

**[Text overlay]:** "$2,400 x 6 = $14,400"

"Fourteen thousand four hundred dollars. And look — the facials felt amazing. I'm not saying they're a scam."

**[Lean in, lower voice slightly]**

"But here's what my esthetician finally told me. She said: 'The LED panel I use on you for 10 minutes? You can buy one and use it at home every single day.'"

**[Pause for effect]**

"She literally told me to stop coming so often."

---

## CLOSE (40-55 seconds)
**[Hold up or gesture to the panel/mask]**

"A clinical-grade LED panel costs... one month of facials. And you can use it every day for years."

**[Text overlay]:** "1 panel = 1 month of facials. Use it for years."

"I still get a facial sometimes as a treat. But the daily maintenance? That's done at home now."

**[Casual shrug]**

"Just wish someone had told me six years ago."

---

## FILMING NOTES
- Couch, bed, or anywhere casual — storytime format should feel like a FaceTime call
- Selfie mode, natural light from a window to your side
- Use TikTok's text overlay tool to add the dollar amounts AFTER filming
- Let your genuine reactions show — the "$14,400" realization should feel real
- One take. Pauses and "um"s are fine. Authenticity > polish.

## AUDIO
Original audio only. Storytimes with your own voice outperform music by 3x.
