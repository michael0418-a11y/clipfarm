## SEQUENCE (film in order)

**Shot 1 (0-5 sec):**
Close-up of hands opening the mask case or box.
Let the click/snap of the case opening be audible.
**[Text overlay]:** "the most satisfying skincare step you've never tried"

**Shot 2 (5-10 sec):**
Lift the mask out slowly. Show the weight of it in your hands.
Let the material sound be heard — the soft silicone, the strap adjusting.

**Shot 3 (10-15 sec):**
Close-up of your finger pressing the power button.
The LEDs turn on. Hold on this moment — the red glow appearing.

**Shot 4 (15-25 sec):**
Dim room or dark room. Place the mask on your face slowly.
The red glow illuminates the room. Hold this shot for 5-10 seconds.
Let the ambient hum of the device be the only sound.

**Shot 5 (25-35 sec):**
You lying back, mask on, completely relaxed.
Soft breathing. Maybe a candle or ambient light in the background.
Hold for 5 seconds.

**Shot 6 (35-45 sec):**
Remove the mask slowly. Soft smile. Touch your face gently.
Camera close-up on your skin.
**[Text overlay]:** "10 minutes. Every night."

## FILMING NOTES
- Film at NIGHT or in a dark room — the LED glow is the star
- Phone on a stack of books or propped on a nightstand
- SILENCE IS KEY — no music, no fan, no TV in background
- The room should be dead quiet so every product sound is crisp
- Multiple angles: use two takes (one from front, one from side) and cut together
- The dark room + red glow shot is the scroll-stopper — make sure it's DARK
- Post-processing: none needed. Raw ASMR performs best.
