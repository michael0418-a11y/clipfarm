HOW TO VIEW THEM
================
Just double-click the index.html file in either folder.
It opens right in your browser — no install needed.

HOW TO CUSTOMIZE FOR A CLIENT
=============================
1. Open the index.html in any text editor (even Notepad)
2. Find-and-replace the business name (e.g., "Bella's Italian Kitchen")
3. Change the address, phone number, and hours
4. Update the menu items and prices
5. Save the file — done!

Or just ask me (Claude) to customize it for you. Say something like:
  "Customize the restaurant template for Mario's Pizza at 456 Oak St,
   phone 555-999-8888, open Mon-Sat 10am-11pm"

HOW TO HOST FOR FREE (GitHub Pages)
====================================
1. Go to github.com and create a free account (if you don't have one)
2. Click "New Repository"
3. Name it something like "marios-pizza" (the client's business)
4. Upload the index.html and style.css files
5. Go to Settings > Pages > Source: main branch
6. Your site is live at: yourusername.github.io/marios-pizza

OTHER FREE HOSTING OPTIONS
===========================
- Netlify (netlify.com) — drag and drop your folder, instant site
- Vercel (vercel.com) — same idea, also free
- Cloudflare Pages — free, very fast

HOW TO FIND CLIENTS
====================
1. Open Google Maps
2. Search "plumbers near me" or "restaurants near me" etc.
3. Look for businesses WITHOUT a website link
4. Call or visit them and show them the demo
5. Your pitch: "I can have a site like this live for you in 48 hours"
