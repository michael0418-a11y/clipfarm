WEB AGENCY CRM
==============

HOW TO START
------------
Double-click START.bat
Then open: http://localhost:3030

FIRST TIME SETUP
----------------
1. You'll be asked for your Anthropic API key
2. Get it free at: https://console.anthropic.com/settings/keys
3. Paste it in and click Save

WHAT IT DOES
------------
- Find Leads: AI finds local businesses with no website
- My Leads: Save and track every business you contact  
- Build Website: Generate a full demo site in 30 seconds

Your leads are saved in: data/leads.json
Generated sites are saved in: generated-sites/
