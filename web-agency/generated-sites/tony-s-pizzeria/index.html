Our Story

For over 15 years, Tony's Pizzeria has been serving the community with authentic Italian cuisine made from scratch daily. Our recipes come straight from Nonna Tony's's kitchen in Napoli.

We use only the freshest local ingredients, imported Italian cheeses, and hand-made pasta to bring you a true taste of Italy — right here in your neighborhood.
