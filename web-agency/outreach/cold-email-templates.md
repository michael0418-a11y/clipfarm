## TIPS FOR SENDING:
1. Send emails between 9-11 AM local time (Tue-Thu works best)
2. Personalize the first sentence — mention something specific about their business
3. ALWAYS include a link to a demo site so they can see your work
4. Follow up once after 3-4 days, then move on
5. Find email addresses on their Google Business listing, Facebook page, or Yelp
6. If no email, message them on Facebook or Instagram instead
