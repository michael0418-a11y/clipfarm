# Lead Finding Action Plan — Do This TODAY

## STEP 1: Find Businesses Without Websites (30 minutes)

Open Google Maps and search for these in your city:

1. "barber shop [your city]"
2. "restaurant [your city]"
3. "lawn care [your city]"
4. "nail salon [your city]"
5. "auto repair [your city]"
6. "plumber [your city]"
7. "cleaning service [your city]"
8. "tattoo shop [your city]"

For each result:
- Click on the business listing
- Look for "Website" — if there's NO website link, that's your lead
- If they have a website, click it — if it looks terrible or isn't mobile-friendly, that's also a lead
- Write down: Business Name, Phone, Address, Owner Name (if visible), what they need

**Goal: Find 20 leads in 30 minutes**

## STEP 2: Build Custom Demos (2-3 hours)

Pick the top 5 leads and customize a demo for each:
1. Copy one of your existing templates (barber, restaurant, lawn care)
2. Swap in their business name, phone number, address, and hours
3. Deploy to Netlify (free)
4. Now you have a LIVE link to show them

**This is your secret weapon.** Showing someone a website with THEIR name on it is 10x more effective than showing a generic portfolio.

## STEP 3: Make Contact (1-2 hours)

For each of your 5 custom demos, do ONE of these (in order of effectiveness):

### Option A: Walk In (BEST conversion rate)
- Walk in with your phone, show them their demo
- Use the pitch script in walk-in-pitch-script.md
- Best for: barbers, restaurants, salons, retail shops

### Option B: Cold Email
- Find their email on Google Business, Facebook, or Yelp
- Use Email Template #1 from cold-email-templates.md
- Include the live demo link
- Best for: any business with an email listed

### Option C: Facebook/Instagram DM
- Find their Facebook or Instagram page
- Send a short message with their demo link:

> "Hi! I build websites for local businesses and I put together a free demo for [Business Name] — check it out: [link]. If you're interested, I can have it live on Google within 24 hours. No charge for the demo!"

### Option D: Phone Call
- Call during business hours
- Keep it short: "Hi, I'm Micha, I build websites for local businesses. I noticed you don't have one yet so I put together a free demo — can I text you the link?"

## STEP 4: Follow Up (ongoing)

- If no reply after 3-4 days, follow up ONCE
- Then move on to the next lead
- Keep a spreadsheet or use your web-agency dashboard to track leads
