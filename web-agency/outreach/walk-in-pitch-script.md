# Walk-In Pitch Script for Local Businesses

Use this when you physically walk into a business.

---

## THE 30-SECOND PITCH

> "Hey! My name's Micha. I build websites for local businesses around here. I noticed [Business Name] doesn't have a website yet, so I actually went ahead and put a demo together for you — totally free, just to show you what it would look like."

**[Show them the demo on your phone]**

> "It's got your hours, your location, your services — and customers can contact you right from it. If you want it live, I can have it on Google within 24 hours for just $299, one-time. No monthly fees."

Then let THEM talk. Answer questions. Don't be pushy.

---

## COMMON QUESTIONS & HOW TO ANSWER

**"How much does it cost?"**
> "It's $299 for a starter site — that's a one-time payment. No monthly fees from me. You own the website forever."

**"I already have a Facebook page, why do I need a website?"**
> "Facebook is great for social, but when someone Googles '[their service] near me,' Facebook pages rarely show up. A website is what gets you on Google — where 81% of people look before they buy."

**"I'll think about it."**
> "Totally understand! Here's what I'll do — I'll keep your demo live for a week so you can look it over. Here's my number if you have any questions. No pressure at all."

**"Can you make changes?"**
> "Absolutely. I'll customize everything — your colors, your photos, your menu, whatever you need. And after it's live, small updates are just $25-50."

**"Do I have to pay upfront?"**
> "Nope. I build it first, you see it, and you only pay when you're happy with it."

**"What about hosting? Is that extra?"**
> "I set you up on free hosting, so there's literally no monthly cost. If you grow and need something more, hosting is as low as $5/month, but most small businesses are totally fine on the free plan."

---

## WHAT TO BRING
1. Your phone (to show demos)
2. A simple business card or flyer (even a printed paper with your name, number, website URL)
3. Confidence — you built these sites, they look professional, and you're offering real value

## BEST TIMES TO WALK IN
- **Restaurants**: 2-4 PM (between lunch and dinner rush)
- **Barbers/Salons**: Early morning or late afternoon (slower periods)
- **Contractors/Lawn care**: Leave a flyer if they're in the field, follow up by phone
- **Retail shops**: Weekday mornings (Tue-Thu)

## TARGET: 5-10 businesses per day
- You only need 1 out of every 10 to say yes
- That's $299-$499 per sale
- 5 sales = $1,500-$2,500
